GARDENING BASICS

Growing Guide

Growing herbal plants can be a rewarding way to learn about nature. These basic tips are a starting point; always check the needs of each individual plant.

☀️

Sunlight

Understand whether your plant prefers full sun, partial shade or indirect light. Place pots according to their needs.

💧

Watering

Check the top layer of soil before watering. Different plants need different amounts of moisture.

🪴

Soil & Pots

Use suitable potting mix and containers with drainage holes to help prevent waterlogging.

🌾

Plant Nutrition

Use compost appropriately and avoid excessive fertiliser. Follow guidance for each plant.

✂️

Pruning

Remove dead or damaged growth with clean tools when appropriate for the plant.

🔍

Plant Health

Check leaves and stems regularly for pests, unusual spots or signs of stress.

RESPONSIBLE PLANT KNOWLEDGE

Safety & Awareness

Natural does not always mean safe. Some plants can be toxic, cause allergic reactions or interact with medicines.

⚠ Important health notice

This website is an educational mini project, not a medical guide. It does not diagnose, prevent or treat any disease. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before using herbal products for health purposes.

🚫
Do not self-medicateNever replace prescribed medicines or medical care with herbal remedies without professional advice.
🌿
Identify plants correctlySome plants look alike. Never eat or use a plant based only on an uncertain identification.
🤰
Extra care for vulnerable groupsChildren, pregnant or breastfeeding people, and people taking medicines should seek professional advice before using herbal products.
🧴
Be careful with concentrated extractsEssential oils and concentrated plant extracts can cause harm if swallowed or applied incorrectly.
📚
Check reliable informationTraditional use is not the same as proven medical effectiveness. Check reliable botanical and health references.
